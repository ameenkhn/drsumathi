Your Experience Matters

Tell Us How We Did

Patient feedback is how we hold ourselves to the quality and safety standards we have committed to. Whether your stay went well or something fell short, we want to hear it — every response reaches our Hospital Operations Head directly.

It Reaches a Person

Every submission goes to Dr. B. Sumathi, our Hospital Operations Head, not to an unattended inbox.

It Feeds Our Quality Reviews

Feedback is reviewed as part of our NABH quality and infection-control programme, and acted on where needed.

We Get Back to You

If you leave your contact details and ask us to, we will call you back about anything that needs resolving.

What helps us most

  • Which department you visited, and whether it was an admission, day-care surgery, outpatient consultation or dialysis session
  • The approximate date of your visit or discharge, and your IP / OP number if you have it
  • What went well — the care, cleanliness, waiting time or how clearly things were explained
  • What could have been better, in your own words
  • Your name and mobile number if you would like us to call you back — otherwise feel free to stay anonymous

For anything urgent or clinical, please call +91 93603 13970 rather than sending a message.
