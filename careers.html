Build Your Career With Us

Sudar Hospital And Research Institute is a NABH (Entry Level) accredited surgical centre where quality and patient safety are taken seriously. If that is how you like to work, we would like to hear from you — send us your résumé and we will get in touch when a suitable role opens.

Why Sudar Hospital

A Place to Do Good Work

Quality-Led Environment

NABH Entry-Level accredited four times since 2017, with CAHO ACE-CSSD and iCOMPLY certification — protocols you can be proud to work within.

Learn From Experience

Work alongside a surgical team led by Dr. S. Sasiraj, with more than 30,000 surgeries behind it and a pioneering laparoscopy practice.

Multispeciality Exposure

Surgery, ophthalmology, nephrology and dialysis, critical care, diagnostics and CSSD — all on one campus.

Departments

Where We Recruit

We accept résumés on a rolling basis across the following departments. Tell us which one fits you and we will keep your details on file.

Doctors & Consultants

General & laparoscopic surgery, anaesthesiology, nephrology, ophthalmology and duty medical officers.

Nursing

Staff nurses for ward, ICU, operation theatre and dialysis; nursing supervisors and in-charges.

Allied Health & Technicians

Laboratory, radiology and imaging, dialysis technicians, OT technicians and CSSD staff.

Front Office & Billing

Reception, patient registration, insurance and TPA co-ordination, billing and records.

Quality & Infection Control

Quality co-ordinators and infection-control nurses supporting our NABH, ACE-CSSD and iCOMPLY programmes.

Support Services

Pharmacy, housekeeping, biomedical maintenance, security, canteen and transport.
